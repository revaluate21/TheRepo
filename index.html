GPS, compass and offline help

GPS requires HTTPS and a location permission. Compass is optional: when unavailable, the map still uses your GPS course while moving and keeps north up. The app shell and viewed photos are cached after first use. Street-map tiles depend on the network or your browser cache; route lines and pins remain visible if the background tiles drop out.
