DAY VS NIGHT

What changes when the sun is up?

☀️

Daytime Lisbon

Tiles, façades, gardens and interior museums become the main event. It is busier and much easier to orient yourself, but exposed hills and the riverfront can feel brutally hot.

  • Best for Estufa Fria, Carmo interior, Castelo and Aljube.
  • Start early or use the heat-smart route.
  • More people around; less eerie, less cinematic.
🌙

Night-time Lisbon

Quieter streets, warm lighting and the river breeze make the city atmospheric, but interiors close and isolated uphill sections can feel emptier.

  • Use the busy-and-bright route after midnight.
  • Keep one ear free and avoid stopping for unsolicited pitches.
  • The app sends street routing to Google Maps so the phone can stay away.

🛡️ Night awareness

Prefer lit streets with foot traffic, do not advertise a nearly dead battery, and keep enough audio awareness for traffic and people. A simple “não, obrigado” while continuing to walk is enough for unsolicited sellers. Emergency number: 112.

How this version avoids the bad arrow problem

This map stays north-up and shows only your location, the quest order and nearby attractions. It does not pretend that a phone compass knows which alley to take. The large navigation button opens Google Maps with walking mode and dir_action=navigate, so Google handles the pedestrian street graph and turn-by-turn voice guidance.

GPS and offline notes

Arrival detection works while this app is open. Browsers can pause GPS when backgrounded, which is why Google Maps handles the journey while the phone is in your pocket. The app shell and viewed images can be cached; unseen map tiles and live transit still need data.
